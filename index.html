Time Series

Sales Forecasting

Problem A retail company needs accurate 3-month sales forecasts for inventory planning and budget allocation.
Approach Decomposed 3 years of daily data into trend + seasonality. Tested ARIMA, SARIMA (grid search), and Random Forest with lag features. Full residual diagnostics.
Result SARIMA delivered 6.2% MAPE on 12-week holdout. RF with lag features matched performance, offering a non-parametric alternative for complex patterns.
