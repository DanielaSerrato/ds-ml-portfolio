Customer Segmentation.

Segmentacion de Clientes.

Overview.
Descripcion General.

In many e-commerce businesses, all customers receive the same marketing campaigns regardless of their purchasing behavior. This one-size-fits-all approach leads to wasted marketing spend, generic communications that fail to resonate, and — most critically — silent churn from high-value customers who feel underappreciated.

En muchos negocios de e-commerce, todos los clientes reciben las mismas campanas de marketing sin importar su comportamiento de compra. Este enfoque generico genera desperdicio en inversion de marketing, comunicaciones que no conectan y, lo mas critico, abandono silencioso de clientes de alto valor que se sienten poco valorados.

This project applies RFM analysis (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) combined with K-Means clustering to segment 5,000 customers based on 50,000 historical transactions. The goal: identify distinct behavioral groups so the business can allocate resources where they matter most and design targeted retention strategies.

Este proyecto aplica analisis RFM (Recencia, Frecuencia, Valor Monetario) combinado con K-Means clustering para segmentar 5,000 clientes basandose en 50,000 transacciones historicas. El objetivo: identificar grupos de comportamiento distintos para asignar recursos donde mas importan y disenar estrategias de retencion focalizadas.

Understanding who your best customers are — and who is about to leave — is the foundation of data-driven customer strategy. This analysis delivers actionable segments that connect directly to marketing, product, and CRM decisions.

Entender quienes son tus mejores clientes — y quienes estan a punto de irse — es la base de una estrategia de clientes basada en datos. Este analisis entrega segmentos accionables que se conectan directamente con decisiones de marketing, producto y CRM.

Approach.
Metodologia.
  1. Data Preparation: Cleaned 50K transaction records. Removed duplicates, handled missing values, and computed per-customer aggregates across Recency, Frequency, and Monetary dimensions.
  2. Preparacion de Datos: Limpie 50K registros de transacciones. Elimine duplicados, maneje valores faltantes y calcule agregados por cliente en las dimensiones Recencia, Frecuencia y Valor Monetario.
  3. RFM Feature Engineering: Calculated days since last purchase (R), total purchase count (F), and total spend (M). Log-transformed monetary values to reduce skew. Standardized all features with StandardScaler.
  4. Ingenieria de Variables RFM: Calcule dias desde la ultima compra (R), conteo total de compras (F) y gasto total (M). Aplique transformacion logaritmica al valor monetario para reducir sesgo. Estandarice todas las variables con StandardScaler.
  5. Optimal Cluster Selection: Applied Elbow Method (inertia curve) and Silhouette Analysis to determine optimal k. Validated with 4 clusters providing the best separation and interpretability.
  6. Seleccion Optima de Clusters: Aplique el Metodo del Codo (curva de inercia) y Analisis de Silueta para determinar el k optimo. Valide con 4 clusters proporcionando la mejor separacion e interpretabilidad.
  7. K-Means Clustering: Ran K-Means (k=4) with multiple initializations. Assigned each customer to a segment and profiled clusters using radar charts, box plots, and distribution analysis.
  8. Clustering K-Means: Ejecute K-Means (k=4) con multiples inicializaciones. Asigne cada cliente a un segmento y perfile los clusters usando graficos radar, box plots y analisis de distribuciones.
  9. Segment Profiling & Strategy: Named and characterized each segment (Gold, Silver, Bronze, At Risk) with business-specific recommendations for marketing, retention, and upselling strategies.
  10. Perfilamiento de Segmentos y Estrategia: Nombre y caracterice cada segmento (Gold, Silver, Bronze, At Risk) con recomendaciones especificas de negocio para estrategias de marketing, retencion y upselling.
